# ADR-004: First Public Release Scope

- **Status:** Accepted.
- **Date:** 2026-11-16

## Context

The complete long-term vision is broad. Launching every capability at once would delay feedback and increase risk.

## Decision

Launch a complete basic platform: 66-book framework, legally distributable text, Bible reading and search, progressive study content, encyclopedia foundations, cross-references, initial maps and timelines, and personal library features. AI is excluded from the first release. Deliver in small usable stages through private alpha, invited beta and public launch.

## Consequences

- The platform has a useful scope without requiring complete research coverage before launch.
- Translation availability depends on verified permissions.
- Study content and advanced research can expand after the core is stable.
- AI quality can benefit from the mature knowledge and citation infrastructure.
