# 9. Licensing and Attribution

## 9.1 General rule

No Bible translation, dataset, research article, image, map tile, audio or video may be redistributed merely because it is publicly accessible online.

Verify the exact edition, file, licence, permitted use, attribution wording and restrictions before import, display, caching, transformation or AI use.

Permission to quote a translation does not automatically grant permission to redistribute its complete text or use it in AI systems.

## 9.2 Bible translations

Target translations: NIV, KJV and NKJV.

- NIV: obtain and document appropriate permission from the rights holder before redistribution.
- NKJV: obtain and document appropriate permission from the rights holder before redistribution.
- KJV: verify the exact 1769-standardised source edition and jurisdiction. eBible.org describes the text as public domain outside the UK and notes a UK letters-patent restriction concerning printing and import of printed copies. That notice does not, by itself, resolve all digital app distribution questions. Obtain jurisdiction-specific legal review before UK distribution.
- Start with text whose specific licence permits the intended use.
- Keep translation edition, rights holder, licence, permission evidence, territory and permitted display/caching uses in the translation registry.

Permission references to investigate:
- https://www.biblica.com/permissions/
- https://www.harpercollinschristian.com/sales-and-rights/permissions/

## 9.3 Candidate open datasets

Candidates require a current licence and file-level review before use.

- Theographic Bible Metadata: https://github.com/robertrouse/theographic-bible-metadata
- STEP Bible data: https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data
- OpenBible cross-references: https://www.openbible.info/labs/cross-references/
- OpenBible geocoding: https://github.com/openbibleinfo/Bible-Geocoding-Data
- Free Use Bible API: https://bible.helloao.org/docs/
- USX Scripture interchange format: https://ubsicap.github.io/usx/

Do not assume all data exposed by an API shares one licence. Record the licence and terms for each dataset/file/translation.

## 9.4 Attribution

Use record-level attribution plus a central licence registry. Each imported or externally sourced record should identify source, version, licence, attribution text, retrieval date and transformations where relevant.

Maintain a central credits/licences page, but do not rely on it as a substitute for attribution on individual records where required.

## 9.5 Research and media

Check individual rights for articles, PDFs, quotations, photographs, diagrams, audio, video and maps. Store only material permitted by its licence or explicit permission. Where redistribution is not permitted, store metadata and a link/citation rather than the protected content.

## 9.6 AI use

Display rights and AI-use permissions must be evaluated separately. Do not assume permission to display a work includes permission to ingest it for model training, embeddings, retrieval or generated summaries. Retain source and rights metadata for AI retrieval.

## 9.7 Repository licence

No repository-wide licence has been selected yet. Do not add an open-source licence until the owner chooses one and confirms which documentation/content it covers.
